📐 Example: Real Analysis Theorem

Theorem: Continuity implies Boundedness on Closed Intervals

Statement: If f is continuous on the closed interval [a, b], then f is bounded on [a, b].

Proof Strategy (AI Suggested):

  1. Assume by contradiction: Suppose f is not bounded on [a, b]
  2. Construct sequence: For each n, choose xₙ ∈ [a, b] such that |f(xₙ)| > n
  3. Apply Bolzano-Weierstrass: Extract convergent subsequence xₙₖ → x₀
  4. Use continuity: f(xₙₖ) → f(x₀) by continuity at x₀
  5. Reach contradiction: But |f(xₙₖ)| > nₖ → ∞, contradicting convergence
